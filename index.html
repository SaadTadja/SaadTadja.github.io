01 — About

From the real need to the running system

I build AI/ML solutions end to end — starting from the actual business need and finishing in production. In practice that means LLM agents driven by careful prompting, machine-learning pipelines that monitor and retrain themselves, and the APIs, data models and interfaces that let real users act on the output.

My degree sits between industrial engineering and data: operations and supply chain on one side, machine learning, information systems and generative AI on the other, after two years of CPGE MPSI/MP. That mix is why I care as much about reliability, explainability and testing as about model accuracy — a prediction nobody trusts is not a delivered system.
